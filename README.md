# Life Organizer ✦

A personal productivity app (PWA) to plan your day, get your work done, and stay motivated. Works on phone and desktop, and offline after the first visit.

## Features
- **Today dashboard**: gradient hero with greeting, daily goal ring, and a motivational quote of the day (tap for another).
- **Motivation system**: 🔥 day streak, ⭐ XP for every task (High +15, Medium +10, Low +5), levels from *Starter* to *Legend*, confetti, and a celebration when you hit your daily goal or level up.
- **Up next**: the event happening now or coming up, with a countdown.
- **Tasks**: priority, due date and time, color-coded categories, notes, filters, quick add.
- **Schedule**: weekly view (starts Sunday); events can be one-off, daily, weekly, or workdays (Sun–Thu), with colors and locations.
- **Focus timer**: Pomodoro (25 / 5 / 15) linked to a task. Each completed session gives +20 XP, plays a chime, and sends a notification.
- **Weekly chart**: tasks completed over the last 7 days.
- Gregorian + Hijri date, light and dark mode, undo, JSON export/import.

Data is stored on your device only (localStorage).

## Run locally
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

## Install on your phone
1. Enable GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Branch: `main`).
2. Open the link on your phone → "Add to Home Screen".
